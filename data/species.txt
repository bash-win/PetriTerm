# --- Omnivores ---
# Each sits in ground no herbivore has its ideal anywhere near - the jungle, the
# wetland and the tundra are the parts of a generated map nothing else browses. That
# is the whole reason they persist. An omnivore placed in temperate grassland is a
# strictly worse rabbit that also eats rabbits: it grazes the same plants less
# efficiently, so competition removes it, and where it wins instead it removes the
# grazers. There is no setting of its numbers that avoids both, which is why this is
# a matter of where they live rather than of what they are worth.
#
# Three of them, in three climates that fail under different conditions. Two was not
# enough while both were humid: a seed whose map happens to come out dry takes the
# jungle and the wetland together, and with them the whole trophic level. The tundra
# is the third piece of ground no herbivore has its ideal near, and it is dry, so a
# map that starves the first two is a map the third does well on.
#
# The cold one also gives the wolf a prey base in its own climate. Nothing else the
# wolf eats has its ideal anywhere near freezing, so the tundra was a predator
# hunting a herbivore population that only reached it at the edge of its tolerance.
